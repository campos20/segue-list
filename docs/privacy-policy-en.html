Segue List Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026 · Ler em português

Segue List is an app for musicians to organize song lyrics and setlists. This policy is short because the answer is short: the app does not collect, transmit, or share personal data with anyone, including the developer.

What data the app uses

The songs, lyrics, and setlists you create are saved only as local files on your own device's storage, using Expo's file system APIs. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it (for example, generating a backup file or sending a setlist to another musician) — in that case, you pick the destination through your operating system's native share sheet (iOS/Android).

What the app does not do

  • No account, login, or user registration.
  • No server or backend of its own: the app works fully offline.
  • No analytics, usage tracking, advertising, or third-party crash reporting libraries.
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • The app does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifiers.

Device permissions

The app only requests file access for actions you explicitly start: importing a lyrics file (.txt, .docx, .odt, .pdf) through the system's document picker, or exporting/sharing a backup of your library. None of this happens in the background.

Data deletion

Since everything is saved only on your device, deleting a song, a setlist, or uninstalling the app removes that data permanently — there is no server-side copy to delete.

Children

Segue List is not made exclusively for children, but its content is neutral (organizing song lyrics) and can be used by anyone who can read, including children. Because the app does not collect, store on a server, or transmit any personal data from any user — adult or child — there is no collection, use, or sharing of children's data under any circumstance.

Commitment to the Google Play Families Policy

The developer has reviewed the app against the requirements of the Google Play Families Policy and commits to following it. This is consistent with the rest of this policy: because Segue List does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from any user, it is also compliant with children's privacy laws such as COPPA (US) and GDPR (EU), since there is no child data to protect in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this same page, with the updated date revised at the top.
